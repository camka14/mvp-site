Escalades Basketball Club

AAU Basketball Organization

Escalades Basketball Club is a comprehensive and competitive program providing multiple hours of instruction each week for players of all abilities.

We offer full-time basketball teams as well as skills development clinics for girls and boys, ages 8 to 17.

Our team practices and clinics are all located in Manhattan - Upper East and West Side, Midtown and West Harlem - and are scheduled to offer multiple opportunities each week.
