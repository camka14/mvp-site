Scorpions Academy

New York Scorpions Academy teams are the club's foundational, growth-focused program for younger players, typically 8U-13U.

2026 Academy Season Timeline: August and October/November tryouts; January-March winter workouts; April-June spring season; July-August summer season.

Practice locations include Wantagh Park, Cantiague Park, Eisenhower Park, LI Sports Dome, Mayhem Practice Facility, Sports Alley, and Baseball Plus.

We are not currently accepting registrations for this program.
