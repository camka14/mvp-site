CROSS COURT PICKLEBALL IS the first and only dedicated indoor pickleball facility in Westchester County, New York.

Cross Court Pickleball is an inclusive, community-centered club for players of all ages and abilities. Competitive open play, beginner to advanced instruction, adult and junior clinics, or just a fun social experience – Cross Court has something for everyone.

Memberships are required to play or participate in any program offered at Cross Court Pickleball.
