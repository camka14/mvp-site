Baseball

The Town of Brookhaven Youth Baseball Program offers Summer and Fall leagues for youth teams ages 8 to 18 as well as Spring and Fall tournaments. Games are played on artificial turf and grass fields located throughout the Town of Brookhaven.

Leagues

2021 Summer Leagues

The 2021 Summer League starts on June 4th. Games are played on weekday evenings with 6:00 pm/8:30 pm start times and weekends.

2021 Fall League

The 2021 Fall League starts September 17th. Games are played on weekends with occasional weekdays, four weeks of double headers, and playoffs.

Tournaments

State-of-the-art turf fields, a 3 game minimum, live Game Changer scoring, and $875 per team.

  • First Pitch Classic — April 30th - May 2nd
  • Pre-Season Classic — May 21st - May 23rd
  • Labor Day Special (8u - 12u only) — September 3rd - September 5th
  • September Showdown (Wood Bat) — September 10th - September 12th
  • Tournament of Champions — October 22nd - October 24th

Please visit League Lineup or call 631-451-6120 for more information, including registering a team.
