Impact Youth League NYC

The official youth developmental league of NYC & NJ!

The Impact Youth League is a nonprofit organization devoted to improving kids' basketball skills and providing a welcoming platform for them to showcase their talents.

Summer Location: Morningside Basketball Courts, W 118th Street and Morningside Avenue, New York, NY 10027

Our program includes basketball clinics, competitive youth league play, and All-Star programming.
