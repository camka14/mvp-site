New York Soccer Club

Founded in 2009, New York Soccer Club is a youth and amateur soccer club based in Westchester County, NY, USA.

New York Soccer Club is a member of MLS Next and Girls Academy League, with teams competing in EDP and NYCFC-affiliate leagues.
