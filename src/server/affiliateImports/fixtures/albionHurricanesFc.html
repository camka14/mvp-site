Youth Soccer Club in Houston, TX

Albion Hurricanes FC is a youth soccer club in Houston, Texas, providing recreational, competitive, and elite soccer programs for boys and girls ages 6–19. Players can join through seasonal soccer tryouts, recreational registration, camps, and year-round training at multiple Greater Houston locations, including Katy, Cypress, Sugar Land, and Beaumont.


With more than 25 years of experience developing youth soccer players, AHFC offers elite coaching, structured player pathways, college recruitment support, and a positive environment where athletes can progress from recreational soccer to competitive and elite levels.

ABOUT Albion Hurricanes FC

Every player is unique, therefore AHFC provides a development pathway for each player with no limitations other than their own ability and aspirations. Our primary goal is to help shape the character of our players, while providing unparalleled opportunities for future college and professional players through our elite youth program to AHFC Royals. AHFC is more than a soccer club, it's a community.

MISSION

To produce hard working, skillful, technical players employing the following three phases: Foundation, Development and Advancement.

VISION

Our vision is to create a world class, successful, soccer club for all the communities and platform levels and abilities we serve. In addition, our ambition is to create the best “youth to pro” soccer club in the US, with clear links and connection to our community.

AHFC Evaluation Tips

April 7, 2026
AHFC May Evaluations Are Here! Getting ready for evaluations? Here are a few simple tips to help you perform at your best: Hydrate early and often In the Texas heat, hydration is key. It doesn’t start the day of evaluations - it starts days before. Make sure you’re consistently drinking water leading up to your evaluations, so your body is ready to perform. Get a good night’s rest Sleep plays a huge role in both your physical performance and mental sharpness. A well-rested player reacts quicker, thinks clearer, and performs better on the field. Control what you can control You can’t control everything at evaluations, but you can control your effort, attitude, and energy. Coaches notice players who compete, stay positive, and give their best in every moment. Keep it simple and play your game Evaluations aren’t about doing something flashy—they’re about showing who you are as a player. Focus on your strengths, make smart decisions, and play with confidence. This is your opportunity to show what you can do. Sign up today and take the next step with AHFC.
