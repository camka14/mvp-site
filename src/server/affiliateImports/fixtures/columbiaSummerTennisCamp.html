Train Where the Lions Play

The Columbia Summer Tennis Camp is situated at the Milstein Family Tennis Center, home of the nationally renowned Columbia men’s and women’s tennis programs. The camp offers expertise to all players and includes technique and movement, strength, conditioning, speed and agility training, intensive drills, individual instruction and match play.

The camp operates from June 15th to August 14th for weekly and daily sessions. Ages 6-17. All levels welcome.
